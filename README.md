**How I use GitHub**

I fork repositories that inspire me: agent skills, design systems, tooling from people who think
carefully about how AI should work. I read them, try them, and some of them grow into
repositories of my own. So this account has two layers: the forks are my reading list and
toolkit, the repositories I author are what I build with them. Most of what I build is private
(client sites, content and data operations, contest entries); the public ones below show the
approach.
